## 1. The App

Travel Safe currently provides:

- a **neighbourhood safety map** of the Cape Town metro with a colour overlay and legend
- optional **live location** on the map (with your permission)
- **trusted contacts** stored on your device (up to five)
- **Emergency SOS** with a cancel countdown, then SMS alerts that can include your location, plus live updates while the session is active

Features that are **not** in this version — and that these terms do not promise — include safer-route guidance, live crime or official incident feeds, emergency-service directories, city-wide alerts, and crowdsourced local spots.

The overlay is **in-app neighbourhood scores**, not police statistics and not a guarantee of safety.
